Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Who uses this app

Moon's Claw is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

Google user data

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Google Calendar events, Gmail messages and drafts, and Google Drive files (read-only for Drive) to answer the owner's own requests: listing and creating calendar events, reading and drafting email, and reading documents.

  • Data is processed on the owner's computer. Content needed to answer a request is sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) the owner has chosen, under that provider's terms.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with other parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
